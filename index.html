Chapter the First

I

Das Bankett

« Dis-moi ce que tu manges, je te dirai ce que tu es. » (Tell me what kind of food you eat, and I will tell you what kind of man you are.) — Jean Anthelme Brillat-Savarin, Physiologie du Goût

Das Abendmahl

A pâté worthy of her beauty is served each evening. Stir it gently in its crystal vessel. For the prosperity of the empire, add ⅛ tsp Lysine and ½ scoop Proden PlaqueOff (the small scoop packed with it).

Trockene Gaben

The supply of dry delicacies is replenished by unseen magic. A weekly polishing of her serving dish is essential.

Nektar der Götter

Churu: offer this ambrosia as a token of adoration.

Katzenminze (catnip): a sprinkle of this euphoric herb shall send Her Majesty into joyous reverie.

Both sacred offerings are secreted within the lower left drawer of the grand media cabinet.

Der Lebensbrunnen

Her royal water fountain must be renewed every two days.

Caution: the top and base of the fountain are not connected; handle with care.

Kaiserliche Zerstreuungen

« Quand je me joue à ma chatte, qui sait si elle passe son temps de moi plus que je ne fais d'elle ? » (When I play with my cat who knows whether I do not make her more sport than she makes me?) — Michel de Montaigne, Essais

Die Jagd nach dem Sternenlicht

Capture a beam of light at dusk and let it dance like a firefly through her chambers.

Spiele der Musen

Let small feathers and shining orbs bounce from the furniture like notes in a symphony. It is your sacred duty to delight Her Majesty.

Chapter the Third

III

Der Kaiserliche Mantel

Her coat requires your most delicate touch. The green brush smooths the strands, while the blue comb resolves any tiny imperfection.

Die Fenster zur Seele

Her eyes, twin emeralds of startling clarity, must be free of any blemish. A gentle touch ensures her gaze continues to enchant.

Should anything about her eyes seem amiss, summon the k. u. k. Leibärzte.

Die Wacht am Rücken

Although rare, under certain circumstances her rear may be compromised. Cleansing wipes befitting her station are located in the lower left cabinet adjacent to the range.

Should the trouble persist or recur, summon the k. u. k. Leibärzte.

Rite of the 5-Day Renewal

  1. Heed the azure beacon, should it flash upon the panel of command
  2. Withdraw the drawer of offerings by its gilded step
  3. Draw the string and cast out the old tribute
  4. Dress the drawer in a pure new liner, pressed flat lest the all-seeing OmniSense be deceived
  5. Replenish the sacred sands to the raised line of purification
  6. Return the drawer and press the button of renewal

Die Kaiserliche Unterbrechung

Should the Empress interrupt the cycle, delicately extricate Her Majesty to an alternative salon, securely close the doors, and await completion. The Fourth Throne's QuietSift mechanism pauses in deference to her presence.
