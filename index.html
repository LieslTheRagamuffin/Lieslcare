Eine Hommage an die Schönheit von Kaiserin Liesl

Compiled for the faithful of her court, that no comfort be left to chance

„Es ist doch etwas Schönes, Herrliches, Erhabenes um das Leben!" (There is truly something beautiful, magnificent, sublime about life!) — E. T. A. Hoffmann, Lebens-Ansichten des Katers Murr · set down, fittingly, in a cat's own memoir

Chapter the First

I

Das Bankett

« Dis-moi ce que tu manges, je te dirai ce que tu es. » (Tell me what you eat, and I shall tell you what you are.) — Brillat-Savarin, Physiologie du Goût · she eats, therefore, like an empress

Das Abendmahl

A pâté worthy of her beauty is served each evening. Stir it gently in its crystal vessel, and for the prosperity of the empire fold in ⅛ tsp Lysine and ½ measuring cup Proden PlaqueOff — thoroughly, so no grain escapes her notice.

Trockene Gaben

The supply of dry delicacies is replenished by unseen magic. A weekly polishing of her serving dish is essential to the dignity of the table.

Nektar der Götter

Churu: offer this ambrosia as a token of adoration.

Katzenminze (catnip): a sprinkle of this euphoric herb shall send Her Majesty into joyous reverie.

Both sacred offerings are secreted within the lower left drawer of the grand media cabinet.

Der Lebensbrunnen

Her royal water fountain must be renewed every two days.

Caution: the top and base of the fountain are not connected — lift with both hands and due reverence.

Chapter the Second

II

Kaiserliche Zerstreuungen

« Quand je me joue à ma chatte, qui sait si elle ne passe pas son temps de moi plus que je ne fais d'elle ? » (When I play with my cat, who knows whether she is not amusing herself with me more than I with her?) — Michel de Montaigne, Essais · the only question of state she permits

Die Jagd nach dem Sternenlicht

Capture a beam of light at dusk and let it dance like a firefly through her chambers. She will pursue it as her ancestors pursued empires.

Spiele der Musen

Let small feathers and shining orbs bounce from the furniture like notes in a symphony. It is your sacred duty to delight Her Majesty — and, per Montaigne, hers to indulge you.

Chapter the Third

III

Die Rituale der Schönheit

« Les Chinois voient l'heure dans l'œil des chats. » (The Chinese tell the hour in the eyes of cats.) — Baudelaire, Le Spleen de Paris · in her emerald gaze, it is always the golden hour

Der Kaiserliche Mantel

Her coat requires your most delicate touch. The green brush smooths the strands; the blue comb resolves any tiny imperfection.

Die Fenster zur Seele

Her eyes, twin emeralds of startling clarity, must be free of any blemish. A gentle touch ensures her gaze continues to enchant.

Die Wacht am Rücken

Although rare, under certain circumstances her rear may be compromised. Cleansing wipes befitting her station await in the lower left cabinet adjacent to the range.

Chapter the Fourth

IV

Die Alchemie

Der Vierte Thron · Litter-Robot 4

« Sic transit gloria mundi. » (Thus passes the glory of the world — every fifth day, into the drawer below.) — antiphon of coronations, here most literally observed

Rite of the 5-Day Renewal

  1. Heed the azure beacon, should it flash upon the panel of command
  2. Withdraw the drawer of offerings by its gilded step
  3. Draw the string and cast out the old tribute
  4. Dress the drawer in a pure new liner, pressed flat lest the all-seeing OmniSense be deceived
  5. Replenish the sacred sands to the raised line of purification
  6. Return the drawer and press the button of renewal

Die Kaiserliche Unterbrechung

Should the Empress interrupt the cycle, delicately extricate Her Majesty to an alternative salon, securely close the doors, and await completion. The Fourth Throne's QuietSift mechanism pauses in deference to her presence.
